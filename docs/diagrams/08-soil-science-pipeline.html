Soil Intelligence Pipeline
From laboratory sample to management response — the scientific core of the platform
STAGE 1Sample acquisition — provenance firstlaboratories · methods · sampling events
Laboratory & method registry
Accredited laboratories and standardized analytical methods are registered before any data arrives, so every measurement carries method provenance.
Geo-referenced sampling
Field sampling events produce samples split into depth layers, each with documented location, date and sampling protocol.
STAGE 2Laboratory analysis — structured measurementslayer-resolved results
Layer-resolved results
Texture fractions, organic carbon, pH and nutrient results are stored per depth layer, each linked to the laboratory and method that produced it.
Visible anomalies
Suspect values carry explicit quality flags rather than silent exclusion — analysts see what was flagged and why.
STAGE 3Scientific processing — soil engine v1.0deterministic · versioned · reproducible
USDA texture triangle
Sand, silt and clay fractions are classified into the standard USDA textural classes, with composition validation against the triangle geometry.
SOC stock computation
Organic carbon concentration × bulk density × layer thickness yields soil organic carbon stock per layer and per profile.
Geodesic geometry
Field areas and distances are computed on the reference ellipsoid (PostGIS + PyProj) — no flat-map distortion at any latitude.
STAGE 4Integrated assessmentwhole-profile view
Soil condition assessment
Texture, SOC stock and soil-cover observations are combined into a per-field soil condition assessment with full input traceability.
Honest gaps
Inputs that are missing are reported as unknown — never interpolated away, never presented as measured fact.
STAGE 5Management responsefrom assessment to action
Nutrient budgeting
Product applications and crop removals are balanced per field, anchored to the measured soil status.
Cover-crop & residue planning
Species selection, planting windows and residue flows are planned to protect and build soil condition.
Operations & scheduling
Erosion assessments, operation templates and dependency-aware schedules turn plans into executable field work.
Consolidated management plans feed the authoritative field-state baseline and the scenario analysis pipeline — see Figure 5 (decision pipeline) and Figure 9 (data governance).
