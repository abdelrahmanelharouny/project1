   114→- Make unsupported outputs unavailable rather than presenting false precision.

   156→

   158→
   159→## A. Data Provider Architecture
   160→

   162→
   163→Support suitable sources such as:
   164→
   165→- Sentinel-2.
   166→- Landsat 8/9.
   167→- MODIS, where temporal resolution is more useful than spatial resolution.
   168→- Other compatible public Earth observation products where justified.
   169→
   170→Prefer authoritative provider APIs and documented data products.
   171→
   172→Potential access methods include:
   173→
   174→- Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem.
   175→- Microsoft Planetary Computer.
   176→- Google Earth Engine, only when its authentication and deployment requirements are explicitly configured.
   177→- Other documented public APIs or STAC-compatible sources.
   178→
   179→Do not introduce multiple providers that produce redundant data without a clear advantage.
   180→
   181→Build a reusable provider interface supporting:
   182→
   183→- Search by field geometry.
   184→- Date-range queries.
   185→- Cloud coverage filtering.
   186→- Product metadata.
   187→- Spatial resolution metadata.
   188→- Acquisition timestamps.
   189→- Asset retrieval.
   190→- Provider-specific error handling.
   191→- Rate limiting.
   192→- Retry policies.
   193→- Caching.
   194→- Provenance.
   195→
   196→## B. Vegetation Indices
   197→
   198→Implement scientifically correct calculations for:
   199→
   200→### NDVI
   201→
   202→\[
   203→NDVI = \frac{NIR-Red}{NIR+Red}
   204→\]
   205→
   206→### EVI
   207→
   208→\[
   209→EVI = G\frac{NIR-Red}{NIR+C_1 Red-C_2 Blue+L}
   210→\]
   211→
   212→Use documented coefficients for the selected sensor/product.
   213→
   214→Also evaluate whether additional indices are useful:
   215→
   216→- SAVI.
   217→- NDMI.
   218→- NDWI.
   219→- NBR where relevant.
   220→
   221→Do not implement indices without the necessary spectral bands.
   222→
   223→Handle:
   224→
   225→- NoData.
   226→- Cloud contamination.
   227→- Shadow contamination.
   228→- Invalid pixels.
   229→- Division by zero.
   230→- Sensor-specific band mappings.
   231→- Reflectance scaling.
   232→- Geospatial reprojection.
   233→
   234→Preserve the original raster metadata.
   235→
   236→## C. Temporal Vegetation Intelligence
   237→
   238→Build time-series capabilities:
   239→
   240→- Field-level NDVI/EVI statistics.
   241→- Monthly and seasonal summaries.
   242→- Vegetation trajectories.
   243→- Historical baselines.
   244→- Anomaly detection.
   245→- Trend detection.
   246→- Spatial distribution summaries.
   247→- Missing-observation awareness.
   248→
   249→Use seasonal baselines rather than naive comparisons across different months.
   250→
   251→Distinguish:
   252→
   253→- Genuine vegetation decline.
   254→- Seasonal dormancy.
   255→- Harvest-related changes.
   256→- Cloud-related gaps.
   257→- Insufficient observation history.
   258→
   259→Never classify low NDVI alone as a crop disease.
   260→
   261→## D. Field-Level Spatial Intelligence
   262→
   263→Support:
   264→
   265→- Field boundary masking.
   266→- Pixel-level validity masks.
   267→- Field-level zonal statistics.
   268→- Percentile distributions.
   269→- Spatial variability summaries.
   270→- Sub-field zone identification where resolution supports it.
   271→
   272→Respect the source resolution.
   273→
   274→A 10-meter satellite product cannot justify arbitrary centimeter-level field predictions.
   275→
   276→Avoid false precision for small fields.
   277→
   278→## E. Vegetation Stress Intelligence
   279→
   280→Build evidence-based vegetation stress signals.
   281→
   282→Combine vegetation indices with relevant environmental context where available:
   283→
   284→- Temperature.
   285→- Rainfall.
   286→- Soil moisture.
   287→- Water availability.
   288→- Evapotranspiration.
   289→- Crop growth stage.
   290→- Planting and harvest dates.
   291→
   292→Distinguish observed vegetation change from inferred possible causes.
   293→
   294→Do not claim drought, disease, nutrient deficiency, or irrigation failure from NDVI alone.
   295→
   296→## F. Frontend
   297→
   298→Add or extend Satellite Intelligence within CropTwist.
   299→
   300→Provide:
   301→
   302→- Field map overlays.
   303→- NDVI/EVI time series.
