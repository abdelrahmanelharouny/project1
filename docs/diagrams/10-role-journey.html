Role enforcement model
authority is scoped per farm, not per account
Per-farm membership RBAC
Roles attach to farm membership. Authority is ordered owner > manager > agronomist > viewer, and the same account can hold different roles on different farms.
Read-only floor
Viewers hold read-only access across every module. Write and execute capabilities require agronomist or above, and heavy engine runs require agronomist or above.
Owner-managed membership
Only farm owners invite members and grant or change roles. Every membership and role change is recorded in the append-only audit trail.
